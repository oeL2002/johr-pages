Privacy policy

rclone, personal single-user deployment. Last updated 1 November 2026.

This application is a personal, single-user deployment of rclone. Its only purpose is to upload encrypted backup archives to the owner's own Google Drive account. It has no other users and offers no service to anyone else.

What it accesses

Google Drive
Only through the drive.file scope, which grants access solely to files this application itself creates. It cannot read, modify or delete any other file in the Drive account.

What it collects

Nothing. No personal data is collected, logged, profiled, or transmitted to any third party. Backup contents are encrypted on the owner's own machine before upload and are not readable by anyone else, including the storage provider.

What it shares

Nothing is shared with any third party.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded backups remain in the owner's own Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately ends the application's access.
